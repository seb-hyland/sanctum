Numerous barriers are encountered before drugs can reach their final delivery location

---

## [!def] pharmacokinetics

Changes in drug concentration as it moves through different compartments of the body

---

---

## [!def] absorption

Process by which a drug enters vascular system

- oral (liquid/pill/capsule), nasal (vapor/aerosol), transdermal, subcutaneous, intramuscular, intravenous

---

---

## [!def] distribution

Dispersion of the drug into fluids/tissues, following [[routes_of_administration#[!def] absorption]]

---

---

## [!def] excretion

Removal of drug from body, typically by the kidneys

---

# Activity

| Route         | Format        | Advantages                                   | Limitations                                                     |
| ------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Intravenous   | Needle        | Direct, fast acting                          | Pain, patient compliance, self-administration difficulty safely |
| Intramuscular | Needle        | Fast transport, target muscle group          | Pain, muscle soreness :(                                        |
| Subcutaneous  | Needle        | Easier self-administration, less painful     | Not targeted, not ideal for targeted delivery                   |
| Intranasal    | Aerosol, swab | Easy access to airways, self-administration  | Hard to target organs, muscle groups                            |
| Transdermal   | Patch         | Very easy to use, compliance                 | Slow-acting, poor diffusion, ineffective                        |
| Rectal        | Suppository   | Some people like it                          | Some people don't like it                                       |
| Oral          | Pill, syrup   | Easy to take, good compliance, easy delivery |                                                                 |
